# Illustrative impact-writing examples — NOT portfolio evidence

> **FICTIONAL WRITING SAMPLES.** Every number and study scenario below was invented to demonstrate what a concise, outcome-led case study *could* look like. No interviews, usability tests, analytics, launches, clinical validations, or measured results are established by these examples. **Do not copy them into the live portfolio, résumé, application, or client-facing materials as factual claims.**

These examples were previously appended to `outcome` fields in `public/content.js`. They are preserved here as *offline editorial references* so the public-facing case studies can separate verified delivery and learning from hypothetical measurements.

## How to replace an example with a real outcome

For each case study, collect an approved source for the claim (analytics export, research report, launch notes, stakeholder confirmation, or a project artifact). Record **who measured it, when, the sample size or baseline, what changed, and what part you personally contributed**. If you cannot verify a number, describe the actual deliverable, your design rationale, what shipped (if known), and what you learned instead. A truthful qualitative outcome is stronger than an invented percentage.
