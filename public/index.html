Hi, I’m Lauren, a product and visual designer in the Orlando area. I’ve designed for construction, healthcare, and travel, which means I’ve spent a lot of time making complicated things feel less intimidating.

I’m the person who asks one more question, sketches three more options, and keeps fiddling with the details until something finally clicks.

When I’m not designing for work, I’m drawing characters, building something ridiculous in a game, or following a side-project idea much further than I meant to.

A NOTE ON AI

Curiosity first. Tools second.

I use AI the way I use a sketchbook: to get unstuck, test an idea, or see a problem from another angle. It's useful for brainstorming, exploring code, and getting a rough thought into something I can react to.

But the interesting part is still the thinking. I decide what matters, question what comes back, and shape the final experience myself. I don't want AI to replace my taste, my drawings, or the messy work of figuring something out. I want it to give me more room to do that work.
