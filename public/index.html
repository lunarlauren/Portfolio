03 / LET'S CONNECT

Curious by default.
Intentional by design.

Hey, I'm Lauren! I'm a visual and product designer based just outside Orlando. I've worked on everything from construction software to cruise ship apps, and I especially enjoy projects where the information is complicated but the experience doesn't have to be.

I tend to notice the small things: a label that makes someone hesitate, a screen that asks too much at once, or a tiny interaction that makes a task feel easier. I like finding a practical solution without losing the personality of the work.

Outside of work, I'm usually drawing, building something in a game, or getting a little too invested in a side project. I like making things just to see what happens.
