Hey, I'm Lauren! I'm a visual, web, and product designer based in Longwood, Florida, just outside Orlando. I work locally and remotely, making everything from digital products and UX/UI to branding and marketing design.

I like taking complicated things and making them feel simple, intuitive, and a little more fun. I'm big on the details—good typography, thoughtful interactions, and those tiny touches that make something feel like it has a personality.

When I'm not designing for work, I'm probably illustrating, building something in a game, or getting way too invested in a personal project. I like having a reason to try something new.
