About the work

Outside client briefs, I use personal illustration as a place to explore character, expression, and visual style. My role is independent creative direction and illustration, rather than product UX for a defined user group. The visual challenge is to make a character or composition readable and distinctive without relying on extra detail. This collection does not have a documented client brief, audience study, or production deadline.
