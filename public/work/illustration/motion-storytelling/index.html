About the work

Construction planning software has relationships and changes over time that are difficult to communicate in a still image. At Hoylu, my documented contribution included a storyboard for an approximately 30-second product explainer. The audience challenge was to help a viewer understand the product idea quickly, without assuming familiarity with its workflows. My role here was visual storytelling and motion-design planning; a completed animation or production handoff has not been confirmed for this case study.
