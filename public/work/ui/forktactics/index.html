About the work

Dinner shouldn't require a fresh round of decision-making every single night. Forktactics began as my attempt to make weekly meal planning feel less like homework. I conceived and iterated on a product that helps people choose meals, review a week of dinners, and shop with less waste. The experience combines preference-based discovery, a weekly menu, budget awareness, and a grocery list that consolidates ingredients used by multiple recipes. It's a personal project I'm still building and refining.
