About the work

Before surgery, people have enough on their minds. They shouldn't have to decipher a portal to find the next step. I worked on a customizable patient portal that allowed medical practices to tailor the experience and share surgery-related documents and preparation information. The UX challenge was to support different practice needs while keeping the patient-facing experience consistent and easy to understand.
