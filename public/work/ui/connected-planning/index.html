About the work

A construction plan is rarely just one plan. Different people need different pieces of information, but those pieces still have to connect. At Hoylu, I worked on the UX and visual structure of connected planning: how someone gets oriented, understands the relationships between items, and follows the detail behind a decision. I worked on how people move between the overall plan and the details behind it.
