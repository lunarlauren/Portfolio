About the work

I contributed UX/UI design to Holland America Line's shipped stateroom TV experience, including onboard information such as Voyage Tracker. The audience was guests navigating from across the room with a remote rather than a mouse or touchscreen. That input constraint shaped the interface: readable information, clear focus states, and a directional path between controls all needed attention.
