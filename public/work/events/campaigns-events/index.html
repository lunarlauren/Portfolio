About the work

At Hoylu, I worked on visual communications for a construction technology audience across marketing and event touchpoints. The challenge changes with the format: a social graphic has seconds to earn attention, while a booth wall must communicate from across a room. Both need a clear message, recognizable brand, and a useful visual hierarchy.
